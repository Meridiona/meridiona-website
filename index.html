What is Meridian?

Meridian is an engineering intelligence workspace that connects project health, execution context, and team updates in one view.

Does anything leave my Mac?

Meridian is designed to keep your delivery context private and under your control.

Who is it for?

For engineering leaders and teams who want to keep delivery moving without adding another reporting ritual.

Does Meridian replace our project tracker?

No. Meridian sits above the tools you already use and makes the delivery story easier to understand.

How does Meridian get context?

It connects the signals your team already creates and turns them into an accountable delivery story.
