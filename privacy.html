Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026 · Meridiona

Meridiona ("we", "us") operates the Meridian desktop app and the meridiona.com website. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

The Meridian app is local-first

Meridian's core product — capturing your day, analyzing your activity, and building your work journal — runs entirely on your own Mac or Windows machine. That data is never uploaded to our servers. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a worklog entry you explicitly review and approve for posting to a project-management tool you've connected (Jira, Linear, GitHub Projects, Azure DevOps, or Trello), and it goes directly from your machine to that tool.

Signing in to the app

If you connect a project-management tool, Meridian uses Google sign-in (via Clerk, our authentication provider) to establish that connection. We receive your email address and account identifier from Clerk to authenticate the session — we do not receive your Google password, and we don't use this sign-in for anything beyond authorizing the app.

This website

meridiona.com uses two third-party services:

  • PostHog — privacy-friendly product analytics (page views, button clicks) so we can see how people use the site. This only runs after you accept the cookie banner; declining or ignoring it means nothing is captured. We do not sell this data or share it with ad networks.
  • Resend — if you submit your email (and optionally a phone number) through a download or waitlist form, we store it with Resend to send you release notes and updates. You can unsubscribe from any email we send.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data. We don't run cross-site ad tracking. We don't read the contents of your work journal — it never reaches us in the first place.

Your choices

  • Decline or revoke analytics consent any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, or contact us and we'll do it for you.
  • Ask us to delete any email/phone number you've given us via a signup form.
  • Uninstall Meridian at any time — since your journal data lives only on your device, uninstalling removes it (back up first if you want to keep it).

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: company@meridiona.com.
